Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 June 2026

Who we are

MP3 Frame Analysis (at mekyle.com) is provided by ReKyle Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company no. 16485405). ReKyle Ltd is the data controller for the processing described below. For any data-protection question, or to exercise your rights, contact kyle.welsworth@pm.me.

What we process

When you upload a file it is sent to our Cloudflare Worker, parsed in memory to count its MPEG-1 Layer III frames, and the result is returned to you. An MP3 can contain personal data (for example a voice recording), so we treat every upload as potentially containing personal data.

We only read the file's structure — the frame headers used to count frames. We never decode or analyse the audio content, and we never process it to identify anyone. Because of this we do not carry out special-category (Article 9) processing such as biometric identification.

Our lawful basis

We rely on legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): providing the frame-counting service you asked for, and keeping it secure and available. We have balanced this against your interests — because the processing is exactly what you requested, minimal, and discarded immediately, we consider it low-risk. You can object to it at any time (see Your rights).

Retention

Your file is discarded as soon as the response is returned. It is never written to disk, stored in a database, logged, or used for any other purpose. Nothing about the audio is retained on our side.

Who else is involved

The service runs on Cloudflare, which hosts the Worker and processes technical request data (such as your IP address) to route and protect the request. Cloudflare acts as our processor, and for its own network-security functions as a controller — see Cloudflare's privacy policy. We use no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and set no cookies.

International transfers

Cloudflare operates globally, so request data may be processed outside the UK. Such transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards — Cloudflare's data-processing agreement incorporates the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your personal data, and to object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Because we keep nothing once your result is returned, most of these have little to act on in practice — but you can exercise any of them, or object, by emailing kyle.welsworth@pm.me.

We make no automated decisions about you. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
